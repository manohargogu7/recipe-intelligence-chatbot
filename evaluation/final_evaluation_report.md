### Evaluation Criteria

1. Answers must be grounded in dataset records.
2. Recipe IDs and original titles must be preserved.
3. Ingredient ranking must be explainable.
4. Dietary restrictions must be enforced.
5. Missing information must be identified.
6. Substitution claims must communicate uncertainty.
7. Follow-up questions must use conversation context.

### Known Limitations

- Advertisement artifacts in ingredient records.
- Some recipes have missing ingredient data.
- Substitution evidence may be insufficient.
- Ingredient quantities are not automatically validated.
- Follow-up context requires further integration.
